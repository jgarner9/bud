In use, Bud is Jason's assistant: he does most of the legwork, decision-making and thinking, and Bud supports rather than drives. He is vibe coding Bud for now (2026-11-15) but wants full insight into its architecture.

**Why:** Jason said so directly while designing Bud Suite.

**How to apply:** Design Bud features to support his thinking (gather, draft, remember, lay out options), not to take decisions over. Keep the architecture doc ([[bud-suite-architecture-doc]]) complete and explanatory, with the reason for every component and decision, so he can steer without reading code.
